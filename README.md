ETHNI-FIT
An Ethnic Wear Matching and Boutique Marketplace

Ethni-Fit is a web-based platform designed to make ethnic wear shopping easier and more convenient for customers. People often attend different occasions 
such as weddings, engagements, festivals and parties, but due to their busy schedules, they may not have enough time to visit multiple shops and find suitable outfits. Additionally, customers who already own ethnic clothing, such as lehengas or sarees, may find it difficult to find exactly matching components like blouses, dupattas and skirts.
To address these challenges, Ethni-Fit provides a platform where customers can specify their requirements based on preferred colours, clothing categories, designs 
and budget. Customers can also upload pictures of their existing outfits to find suitable matching clothing items.
The platform connects customers with registered boutiques, allowing boutique owners to upload pictures of their available products along with their descriptions,
prices and other details. Based on customer requirements, the website helps them discover suitable products from different boutiques. Customers can either order
their required products online or visit the respective boutiques.
The main objective of Ethni-Fit is to save customers’ time and effort in finding suitable ethnic wear while providing boutiques with an online platform to showcase
their collections and reach more customers.
